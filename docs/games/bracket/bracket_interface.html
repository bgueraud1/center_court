How to play

Playing the bracket is simple: every Sunday, you can register for tournaments taking place one week later, usually 6 to 8 days in advance. Player assignments to specific tournaments are based on the world ranking. If you are not sure your rank is high enough, it is better to enter lower tournaments and submit several registrations.

Once the draw is released, you can submit your bracket by predicting the winner of every match, from the first round all the way to the final.

The world ranking is based on the performance of all users over the last 52 tournaments, while also taking into account their 19 best performances of the year. Play regularly to improve your ranking and earn access to the biggest tournaments on tour.
